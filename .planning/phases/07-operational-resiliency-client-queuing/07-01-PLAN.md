  <action>
    1. In `MqttClient.__init__`:
       - Forward `enable_publish_buffer`, `max_publish_buffer_size`, and `buffer_overflow_policy` to `super().__init__`.
       - Keep `self.subscribed_topics` synchronized as a list view/property or updated alongside `self._subscriptions` for backward compatibility with existing tests.
    2. In `MqttClient.subscribe(self, topic: str, **kwargs)`:
       - Record subscription and kwargs via `self.record_subscription(topic, **kwargs)`.
       - Maintain `self.subscribed_topics` list for backward compatibility.
       - If connected, call `self.client.subscribe(topic, qos=qos)`.
    3. In `MqttClient._on_connect`:
       - Iterate through `self.get_subscriptions().items()` (and any legacy entries in `self.subscribed_topics`) and call `self.client.subscribe(topic, qos=sub_kwargs.get('qos', 0))`.
       - Call `self.flush_publish_buffer()` to drain any messages buffered while disconnected.
    4. In `MqttClient.publish`:
       - When `not self.client.is_connected()`:
         - Check `if self.buffer_publish(topic, message, on_confirm=on_confirm, on_error=on_error, **kwargs): return`.
         - Otherwise log error and invoke `on_error(RuntimeError(...))` as before.
