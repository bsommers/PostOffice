# Plan 261103-p5a: Native Per-Target Bounded Queues & Edge Disk Store-and-Forward

- **Goal:** Revisit P3 durability & queue items natively (`NATIVE_REWRITE_PLAN.md:123` and `§7 Decision 5`) with `TargetPublishQueue` (`po_target_queue_t`) and `DiskStoreAndForward` (`po_disk_store_t`) exposed across the C ABI (`include/postoffice.h`) and Python FFI (`src/postoffice/native.py`, `src/postoffice/base_client.py`).
- **Scope:**
  - `native/libpostoffice/src/queue.rs`: `TargetPublishQueue` (`max_messages`, `max_bytes`, `max_age_sec`, `overflow_policy`: `reject` | `drop_oldest`) and `DiskStoreAndForward` (`size_cap_bytes`, wear-aware write batching via `batch_max_messages`/`batch_max_bytes`/`flush_interval_sec`, IEEE CRC32 record framing, and crash recovery truncating torn trailing frames).
  - `include/postoffice.h` & `native/libpostoffice/src/lib.rs`: C ABI exports (`po_target_queue_*`, `po_disk_store_*`).
  - `src/postoffice/native.py` & `src/postoffice/base_client.py`: `NativeTargetQueue`, `NativeDiskStoreAndForward`, and opt-in `disk_store_path` durability in `BaseClient`.
  - `tests/test_phase5_native_queues_and_storage.py`: Hermetic unit tests covering bounded queues, wear-aware batching, torn-frame crash recovery, and `BaseClient` restart durability.
