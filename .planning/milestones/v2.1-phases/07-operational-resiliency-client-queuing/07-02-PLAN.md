  <files>
    src/postoffice/plugins/amqp_client.py

  <action>
    1. In `AmqpClient`:
       - Forward `enable_publish_buffer`, `max_publish_buffer_size`, and `buffer_overflow_policy` to `super().__init__`.
       - In `subscribe(self, topic: str, **kwargs)`:
         - Always call `self.record_subscription(topic, **kwargs)`.
         - If `not self.channel` or `not self.connection`: log warning that subscription is queued for connect and return without dropping.
         - Otherwise schedule `_apply_subscription(topic, kwargs)` via `self.connection.add_callback_threadsafe`.
       - Extract `_apply_subscription(self, topic: str, kwargs: dict)` helper so both `subscribe()` and `_consume_loop()` can invoke it cleanly.
       - In `_consume_loop()`: after `self.channel = self.connection.channel()`, replay all stored subscriptions from `self.get_subscriptions()` and call `self.flush_publish_buffer()` before setting `self._connected_event`.
       - In `publish()`: when `not self.channel`, check `if self.buffer_publish(topic, message, on_confirm=on_confirm, on_error=on_error, **kwargs): return` before falling back to error callback.
    2. In `KafkaClient`:
       - Forward `enable_publish_buffer`, `max_publish_buffer_size`, and `buffer_overflow_policy` to `super().__init__`.
       - In `subscribe(self, topic: str, **kwargs)`:
         - Always call `self.record_subscription(topic, **kwargs)`.
         - Queue `topic` in `self._pending_subscriptions` under `self._subscribe_lock` even if `self.consumer is None` (so pre-`connect()` subscriptions are applied as soon as `_consume_loop` starts).
       - In `connect()`: after initializing `self.producer` and `self.consumer`, call `self.flush_publish_buffer()`.
       - In `publish()`: when `not self.producer`, check `if self.buffer_publish(topic, message, on_confirm=on_confirm, on_error=on_error, **kwargs): return` before falling back to error callback.
  </action>
